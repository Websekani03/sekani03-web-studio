Why I Started Coding

Balancing coding has not been easy, but it has taught me discipline, consistency, and how to keep improving even when I'm tired. Nursing showed me the importance of responsibility and problem solving, while coding gave me a new challenge that pushed me to think creatively and build things from scratch. I started learning web development because I wanted a skill that would allow me to create, grow digitally, and build a future with more freedom and opportunities.

I chose tech because I enjoy the process of learning, building projects, and seeing progress over time. Every day I code, I become more confident in my abilities and more motivated to keep going. My goal is to become a full stack developer, work on real world products, and build a career that combines skill, creativity, and independence while continuing to document my journey from beginner to pro.

Lessons I'm Learning

As I continue learning web development, I'm beginning to understand that growth in tech is not just about writing code. It's also about discipline, patience, problem solving and staying consistent even when things become difficult. Every small project and challenge is teaching me lessons that will help me become a better developer over time.

  • Patience matters when learning new concepts
  • Debugging is a normal part of coding
  • Consistency beats motivation
  • Small daily improvements add up over time
  • Building projects helps me learn faster
  • Mistakes are part of the learning process
  • Discipline is more important than talent
  • Searching for solutions is a skill every developer needs
